This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on February 1, 2026, between:

Landlord: Mr. Venkatesh Rao, S/o Late Krishna Rao, residing at 8, 5th Main, Indiranagar, Bengaluru – 560038 ("Landlord")

Tenant: Ms. Ananya Krishnan, D/o Suresh Krishnan, currently residing at 22, 2nd Cross, Koramangala, Bengaluru – 560034 ("Tenant")

PREMISES

The Landlord agrees to rent to the Tenant the residential property located at:
Flat No. 301, 3rd Floor, Green Valley Apartments
12, 7th Main, HSR Layout, Sector 2
Bengaluru – 560102
("Premises")

SECTION 1: TERM OF AGREEMENT

1.1 This Agreement shall commence on February 15, 2026 and shall continue for a period of eleven (11) months, ending on January 14, 2027.

1.2 This Agreement may be renewed for a further period by mutual written consent of both parties at least thirty (30) days before the expiry of the current term.

1.3 Upon renewal, the rental amount shall be subject to renegotiation.

SECTION 2: RENT

2.1 Monthly Rent: The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of Rs. 18,000 (Rupees Eighteen Thousand only) to the Landlord.

2.2 Payment Date: The rent shall be paid on or before the 5th day of each month.

2.3 Payment Method: Rent shall be paid by bank transfer to the Landlord's account or by cheque.

2.4 Late Payment Penalty: If rent is not paid by the 10th of the month, a late payment fee of Rs. 500 per day shall be charged for each day of delay.

SECTION 3: SECURITY DEPOSIT

3.1 The Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of Rs. 54,000 (three months' rent) to the Landlord.

3.2 The security deposit shall be returned to the Tenant within thirty (30) days of vacating the Premises, subject to deductions for any damage beyond normal wear and tear, unpaid rent, or outstanding utility bills.

3.3 The security deposit shall not bear interest.

SECTION 4: MAINTENANCE AND UTILITIES

4.1 The Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all utility bills including electricity, water, internet, and gas during the period of this Agreement.

4.2 The Tenant shall maintain the Premises in good and clean condition.

4.3 The Tenant shall be responsible for minor repairs up to Rs. 1,000 per occurrence. Major repairs shall be the responsibility of the Landlord.

4.4 The Tenant shall not make any structural changes or alterations to the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

SECTION 5: PERMITTED USE

5.1 The Premises shall be used by the Tenant for residential purposes only.

5.2 A maximum of two (2) persons shall reside in the Premises. The Tenant shall inform the Landlord if any additional occupant joins.

5.3 Pets are not permitted on the Premises.

5.4 Subletting of the Premises is strictly prohibited.

SECTION 6: INSPECTION

6.1 The Landlord or their representative shall have the right to inspect the Premises with forty-eight (48) hours' prior notice.

6.2 In case of emergency, the Landlord may enter the Premises without prior notice.

SECTION 7: TERMINATION

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement before the expiry of the term by providing sixty (60) days' written notice to the other party.

7.2 The Landlord may terminate this Agreement immediately if:
(a) The Tenant fails to pay rent for two (2) consecutive months;
(b) The Tenant uses the Premises for illegal activities;
(c) The Tenant causes significant damage to the Premises.

7.3 Upon termination, the Tenant shall vacate the Premises within the notice period and leave the Premises in its original condition.

SECTION 8: LOCK-IN PERIOD

8.1 A lock-in period of three (3) months applies from the commencement date.

8.2 If the Tenant vacates during the lock-in period, the Tenant shall forfeit the security deposit.

SECTION 9: GOVERNING LAW

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Karnataka and the Registration Act, 1908.

9.2 Any disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts in Bengaluru.
